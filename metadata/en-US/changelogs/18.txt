- The app icon and its display on the Android launcher have been improved.
- Adding an option to ignore battery optimizations. Previously, it was necessary to do this manually. Now, on first launch, the app asks to set it to "Unrestricted" so the background service keeps running while the device is idle.
- Adding a Quick Settings tile that opens an "Auto Light" panel. Tapping the tile allows turning the service on or off and enabling QR or barcode reader mode.
- QR or Barcode Reader Mode pauses brightness adjustments for 120 seconds, allowing manual changes to the screen brightness without being overwritten by the service, and then automatically turns the service back on.
